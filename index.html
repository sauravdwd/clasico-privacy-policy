Privacy Policy

App: Clasico

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Clasico is a shopping application. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information may be collected when you use Clasico, how it may be used, and how you can contact us about your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Clasico, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you create or use an account.
  • Profile information: Your name, phone number, delivery address, city, and PIN code when you provide these details.
  • Order information: Products ordered, order items, quantities, prices, delivery details, order status, and order history.
  • Sign-in information: Information made available through your chosen sign-in method, such as email and password, Google, or Facebook, subject to the relevant provider's settings and permissions.
  • Payment-related information: Payment status and transaction identifiers may be recorded to process or verify orders. Payments may be handled by Razorpay. Clasico does not ask you to provide your payment password or UPI PIN to us.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Process orders and provide order updates.
  • Use delivery information to fulfil orders.
  • Verify payment status and maintain order records.
  • Respond to customer questions and requests.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Authentication Providers

Clasico may offer sign-in using email and password, Google, and Facebook. If you use an external sign-in provider, that provider processes your sign-in according to its own privacy policy and settings. We receive information made available to Clasico as part of the authentication flow.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

Clasico uses third-party services for certain app functions, including Supabase for authentication and app data services, and Razorpay for payment processing. Relevant information may be processed by these providers as needed to provide their services.

We do not describe any third-party service as having access to every piece of your information. The information processed depends on the feature being used and the service configuration.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to address legitimate security or legal concerns.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, order fulfilment, customer support, record keeping, and applicable legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances.
