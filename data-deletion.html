Clasico - Data Deletion Instructions

Clasico respects your privacy. You can contact us to request deletion of your account and personal information associated with our app.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to saurabhsanyal85@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Clasico Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Clasico account and explain that you want your account or personal information deleted. Do not include your password, UPI PIN, or other authentication secrets.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity before processing the request.

What Happens Next?

We will review your request and handle it in accordance with applicable legal requirements and our data-retention obligations. Some order, payment, security, or legal records may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law.

We will communicate with you if additional information is reasonably needed to process your request.
